A January 15, 2001 staff update reports that Galileo and Odyssey took part in the first week of Submarine EdVentures.[1]

The archived Navigation and Operations stacks have modification dates of March 17, 2002, and Core is dated May 27, 2002. The title reflects the submarine program’s first documented use of Galileo, not the date of this version of the controls. The anomalous 2040 timestamp on Tactical is not used.
