Cocoa

The controls were written in Objective-C and Apple’s Cocoa frameowrk for MacOS X. The system's foundation was a modular card system which allowed cards to be developed as independent plugins, which could be mixed and matched to form a particular station.

Objective-C is a professional language for OS engineering. It was much harder for kids to learn and make significant contributions. Consequently, development took much longer.
