CMSC Software History

An archive of the simulator controls and related software developed at the Christa McAuliffe Space Center.

Controls enabled several computers in a lab to connect together and participate in a sci-fi story. But unlike in a video game, the experience was not driven by computer logic. The software simply relayed students’ actions to staff and allowed staff to interact back, providing the illusion of an intelligent computer system. It was one of several multimedia tools for helping staff to tell a story and immerse students in the experience. See Glossary for more background.

Most content was written by high school and junior high students, with contributions from other volunteers. See Programmers for credits.
